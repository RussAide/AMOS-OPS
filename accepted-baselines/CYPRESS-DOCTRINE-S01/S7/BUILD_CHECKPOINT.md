## Controlling CWOP evidence model

The accepted doctrine is represented as six evidence stages:
1. Baseline — pre-Adolbi placement instability and crisis utilization.
2. Resource profile — actual service level, supervision, staffing, and clinical/behavioral support burden.
3. Authorized economics — rate and authorization supporting the required resource model.
4. Stabilization performance — placement continuity, incidents, hospital returns, stability days, length of stay and transition outcome.
5. Value evidence — credible partner/claims/encounter/utilization/alternative-system evidence supporting comparison.
6. Negotiation case — aggregate verified evidence for an authorized strategic CWOP continuum and sustainable rate methodology.

Before / during / outcome evidence is tracked across CWOP episodes, placement breakdowns, ED/hospital use, law-enforcement/transport, placement searches, supervision/staffing/supports, crises, placement/stability days, hospital returns, safe return, step-down/discharge stability and renewed CWOP/placement-search activity.
